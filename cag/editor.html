How to use

  1. Start the editor over the outputs: uv run cag edit (all of outputs/, or pass one character's folder), then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/.
  2. Open sheet… and pick a <set>-sheet.png from under that folder. The editor matches it to its character by contents, draws that brief's height, and saves back to the same folder.
  3. Play to watch the loop. Pause, then pick the frame that jumps: click it in the strip, drag the slider, or step with the ‹ › buttons beside the strip, ⌘ + ← →, or , .
  4. Move the frame by dragging it on the stage, nudging with the arrow keys, or typing an exact x / y offset. The faded figure is the previous frame; line up against it, the floor guide, and the dashed height guide from the brief.
  5. Scale the frame with [ ], ⌘ + ↑ ↓, or by typing a %. It grows and shrinks about the floor point on the centre line, so feet stay planted.
  6. Save sheet overwrites the sheet and rebuilds <set>-proof.gif at the fps shown, then closes the sheet; the result shows on the stage. Frames you moved carry a dot in the strip. If the save fails the sheet stays open with your edits.
