How to use

  1. Start the editor over a character's output folder: uv run python -m cag.cli edit outputs/<character>, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/.
  2. Open sheet… and pick a <set>-sheet.png from that same folder.
  3. Play to watch the loop. Pause, then pick the frame that jumps: click it in the strip, drag the slider, or step with , .
  4. Move the frame by dragging it on the stage, nudging with the arrow keys, or typing an exact x / y offset. The faded figure is the previous frame; line up against it and the floor guide.
  5. Save sheet overwrites the sheet and rebuilds <set>-proof.gif at the fps shown. Frames you moved carry a dot in the strip.
