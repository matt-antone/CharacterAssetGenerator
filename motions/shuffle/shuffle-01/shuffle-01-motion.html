Performance arc

A shuffle running-man. From weight over the character-left foot the figure centres, the arms low by the hips, then lifts the character-right foot at frame 7, shifts over the character-right at 8-9 with the arm up to chest height, and returns over the character-left.

Quick, low heel-toe footwork. Pingpong: frames 0 and 21 are grounded turnarounds.
