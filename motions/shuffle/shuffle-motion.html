Performance arc

Shuffle basic — a running-man / T-step cycle danced light and high on the balls of the feet. The engine is the hips: every weight change starts as a small pelvic drop on the landing foot, and the shoulders answer a beat late. Keep the whole body buoyant; no frame should sit flat and heavy except the two deliberate holds.

Beat 1 (0-7): two fast running-man steps. Frame 0 is the neutral wide stance — plant it, it is the loop's home pose. Anticipation is already gone by frame 1 (shoulder pops character-left); frames 2 and 4 are the airborne halves of the step, so the feet leave the floor before the torso finishes its character-right lean. Land on the character-right foot at 3 and again around 5, each landing absorbed in the knee, not the ankle. Frame 7 is the big airborne extreme of the phrase — the highest point of the cycle.

Beat 2 (8-17): recovery and settle. Weight crosses to character-left through 9, then the two holds at 11 and 15 are the only rests in the cycle — play them as real stops with a tiny residual bounce in the supporting knee, not as frozen frames. The character-right arm rises to a bent guard across 13-15; that arm carries through and stays up while the legs restart.

Beat 3 (18-27): the knee lift and the throw. Character-right knee folds progressively 18→20 (tightest at 20, the pilot) while the weight stacks over character-left. Frame 21 is the hit pose of the cycle — wide, knee at 90, both arms live at chest height. Then the arms swap altitude: character-left arm swings overhead into 23 as the feet cross under (feet together, weight on character-right), and character-right arm answers overhead by 27. Frames 26-27 are the second airborne extreme — the throw at the top of the phrase.

Beat 4 (28-31) and the seam: land out of the throw, drop the character-right arm, and step the weight back toward centre. Frame 31 is airborne again and frame 0 is grounded and wide, so the loop seam IS a landing — treat 31→0 as the last step of the cycle, not a cut. Absorb the landing in frame 0's knees and let the arms settle by the hips on the same beat. If the seam still reads hard, soften frame 31's height rather than changing frame 0.

For the artist agent

  • This is a motion source: it controls motion only. Character scale, proportions, identity, view and prop hand come from the manifest and approved authorities, never from this video.
  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop at 8 fps, 64 frames. Loop seam: needs blend (frame 64 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Grounded frames keep the supporting heel on the canonical baseline; frames marked airborne leave it only through a coherent takeoff, arc and landing.
  • Draw keys first (pink), then pilots (blue), then in-betweens from locked neighbours. The frame notes below are the per-frame instruction set.
