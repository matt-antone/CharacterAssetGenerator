Performance arc

Two bars of bouncy weight-shifting on the balls of the feet, then one big kick-out that opens the whole body, then a settle back into the bounce. Frames 0-9 ride on the character-left foot: the character-right knee lifts and drops on the beat while the heels stay lifted, arms loose and low, the bounce coming from the ankles and knees rather than the hips.

Frame 10 is the anticipation - a dip and a lean over the character-left foot that loads the weight. Frames 12-17 are the action: weight snaps onto the character-right foot, the character-left leg swings across and behind, and both arms fly open and up, the character-right arm sweeping overhead at 16-17. That is the hit of the phrase; draw it big. Frame 18 is the fastest transition in the set, the arm coming down and the weight crossing back.

Frames 19-23 are follow-through and recovery: arms fold back across the body, the torso squares up, and the feet return to the heels-lifted bounce. Frame 21 is the one still beat. The seam is clean - frame 23's rising character-right knee reads straight into frame 0's lifted foot, so the loop reads as one continuous bounce with no hitch.
