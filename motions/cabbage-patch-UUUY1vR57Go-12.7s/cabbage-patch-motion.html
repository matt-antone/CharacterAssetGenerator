Performance arc

The cabbage patch: both hands circle around each other in front of the chest, one full scoop per weight shift, while the torso rocks side to side. Captured slow (source about half this length), 3/4 view.

Frame 0 is the top of the scoop — character-right arm raised above shoulder height, weight on the character-right foot. Frames 1-4 bring the hands down and across to waist height as the weight crosses to the character-left foot. Frames 5-9 are the far side: character-left arm up at chest height, hands continuing the circle, weight held on the character-left foot for five frames — the longest single support in the loop.

Frames 11-15 unwind back to the character-right side and re-raise the character-right arm above the shoulder, so frame 15 arrives in frame 0's shape. The seam is clean. The one thing to protect is the continuity of the circle: it never stops or reverses, it only changes which hand leads. This capture has no pilot frames at all — every frame is travel at the same rate, so no single frame should be drawn as a pose.

For the artist agent

  • This is a motion source: it controls motion only. Character scale, proportions, identity, view and prop hand come from the manifest and approved authorities, never from this video.
  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop at 8 fps, 16 frames. Loop seam: clean (frame 16 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Depth is explicit: every landmark in pts is [x, y, z] with z negative toward the camera and the hips at zero, and each frame carries a depth block plus a cue sentence wherever the legs overlap. Sort bones by mean z and draw the far ones first; never guess which limb is in front.
  • Grounded frames keep the supporting heel on the canonical baseline; frames marked airborne leave it only through a coherent takeoff, arc and landing.
  • Draw keys first (pink), then pilots (blue), then in-betweens from locked neighbours. The frame notes below are the per-frame instruction set.
