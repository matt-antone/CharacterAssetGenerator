Performance arc

Early Elvis leg work. From a centred stance the figure lifts the character-left foot at frame 3 onto the character-right, recentres, then lifts the character-right foot at frame 9 while the arm comes up to chest height with a bent elbow.

A loose, knee-driven swivel; arms stay close. Pingpong: frames 0 and 21 are grounded turnarounds.
