Performance arc

A two-step with a dip in the torso. Count one steps onto the character-left foot, count two brings the weight back to centre, and on each step the ribcage drops toward the standing side before lifting again. The steps are small, flat and unhurried — this is a shuffle in place with the accent living in the upper body.

Forearms stay in front at waist-to-chest height with soft elbows, floating slightly out and back as the torso dips. Keep the knees unlocked throughout and the shoulders level while the ribs do the work.

Loop seam: the cycle opens and closes on the character-left step with both soles in contact, so the last-to-first cut is one more step of the same two-step.
