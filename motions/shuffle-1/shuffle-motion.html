Performance arc

Anticipation: frame 0 opens grounded, weight centred over both feet, up on the ball of the character-right foot, arms low and easy — the pose the loop returns to.

Action: the phrase is a string of quick hops rather than one big jump — five separate airborne beats (frames 8, 10-12, 18, 21-23, 25-26), each landing back on the balls of the feet before gathering for the next. The biggest is 10-12, arms driving all the way overhead; frames 21-23 add a twist, torso leaning character-right with the hips wound character-left against the shoulders. Between hops the weight ping-pongs character-right/character-left with the trailing leg repeatedly swinging behind the standing one (frames 1-7, 24, 27-28). The heel never fully sets down anywhere in the clip — every grounded frame is on the balls of the feet.

Follow-through and loop seam: the phrase settles back to a grounded, low-armed stance by frame 31 (weight over the character-left foot, both feet down, heels lifted) — close enough to frame 0's centred, ball-of-the-right-foot stance that the loop reads as one continuous bounce; both ends of the seam are on the ground, so it needs no mid-air blend.
