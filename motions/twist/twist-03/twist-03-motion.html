Performance arc

A slow teaching Twist. The figure rocks from weight over the character-right foot through centre, lifts the character-left foot at frames 5 and 9, then shifts over the character-left foot at frame 13 with the arm up to shoulder height, the hips twisting against the shoulders.

Pingpong: frames 0 and 28 are grounded turnarounds.
