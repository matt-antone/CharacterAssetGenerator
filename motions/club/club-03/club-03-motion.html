Performance arc

A club body roll into a step. It opens on the character-right foot with the character-left foot lifted and the arm raising above the shoulder, the weight moves onto the character-left foot at frame 5, and the arm sweeps down across the body to hip height by frame 8.

The back half is a centred, low-arm groove over the character-left foot. Pingpong: frames 0 and 21 are the turnarounds; frame 0 has a lifted foot, so hold that knee raised and clear.

  • Playback is loop · out and back at 7 fps, 22 frames. Loop seam: needs blend (frame 21 cuts back to frame 0).
