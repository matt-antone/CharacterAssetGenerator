Performance arc

A hip-hop step-kick basic. The figure stands on the character-right foot with the character-left foot lifted, sets down at frame 2, lifts again and pumps the character-left arm up to shoulder height at frame 5, then centres at frame 8 and shifts over the character-left foot.

Pingpong: frames 0 and 22 are the turnarounds; frame 0 has a lifted foot, so keep the knee up and clear.

  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop · out and back at 11 fps, 23 frames. Loop seam: clean (frame 22 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Depth is explicit: every landmark in pts is [x, y, z] with z negative toward the camera and the hips at zero, and each frame carries a depth block plus a cue sentence wherever the legs overlap. Sort bones by mean z and draw the far ones first; never guess which limb is in front.
