Performance arc

The Smooth Criminal trend: a hop-driven step with the arms held up at chest-to-shoulder height and elbows bent. It opens in the air (frames 0-2), lands on the character-right foot at frame 3, shifts across, then hops again through frames 7-10 before landing on the character-left foot.

Pingpong: frame 0 is an airborne turnaround, the peak of a hop rather than a jump cut. Draw it as a clear hop, both feet off the floor.

For the artist agent

  • This is a motion source: it controls motion only. Character scale, proportions, identity, view and prop hand come from the manifest and approved authorities, never from this video.
  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop · out and back at 10 fps, 19 frames. Loop seam: needs blend (frame 18 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Depth is explicit: every landmark in pts is [x, y, z] with z negative toward the camera and the hips at zero, and each frame carries a depth block plus a cue sentence wherever the legs overlap. Sort bones by mean z and draw the far ones first; never guess which limb is in front.
  • Grounded frames keep the supporting heel on the canonical baseline; frames marked airborne leave it only through a coherent takeoff, arc and landing.
  • Draw keys first (pink), then pilots (blue), then in-betweens from locked neighbours. The frame notes below are the per-frame instruction set.
