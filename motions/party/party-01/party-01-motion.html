Performance arc

A party groove built on a side-to-side weight shift. From weight over the character-left foot the figure centres, rocks onto the character-right foot and briefly lifts the character-left foot at frame 4, then centres and returns to the character-left side, with the character-left arm lifting to chest height by frame 9.

Arms stay loose and low, swinging out with the hips. Pingpong: frames 0 and 22 are both planted turnarounds.

  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop · out and back at 9 fps, 23 frames. Loop seam: needs blend (frame 22 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Depth is explicit: every landmark in pts is [x, y, z] with z negative toward the camera and the hips at zero, and each frame carries a depth block plus a cue sentence wherever the legs overlap. Sort bones by mean z and draw the far ones first; never guess which limb is in front.
