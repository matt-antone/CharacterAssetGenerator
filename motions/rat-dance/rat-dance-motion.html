Performance arc

Anticipation: frame 0 opens on a centred, grounded stance — weight even on both balls of the feet, character-right arm already lifted to chest height, character-left arm resting low. This is the base groove pose the loop returns to.

Action: two hops (frames 3 and 20) punctuate a longer groove of weight shifts side to side, worked entirely on the balls of the feet — the heel never sets down. The trailing leg crosses behind the standing leg repeatedly (frames 9, 11, 20-21, 26, 29-30) as weight passes foot to foot. Shoulders and hips isolate opposite the weighted side — a raised character-left hip/shoulder pairs with weight on the character-right foot and vice versa — while the head snaps to follow whichever arm crosses the body, giving the groove its bounce and swagger.

Follow-through and loop seam: the phrase resolves back toward a centred stance by frame 31, but the arms land in a mirrored variant of frame 0 (character-left arm chest-high, character-right low) rather than an exact match — blend the last beat into the first so the arm swap reads as part of the groove, not a pop.
