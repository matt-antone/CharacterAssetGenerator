Performance arc

A Beyonce hip-driven groove with the arms held out. Weight stays centred while the arms work at chest-to-shoulder height out to the side, elbows opening and closing, peaking at shoulder height at frames 6 and 13; the weight moves over the character-left foot at frame 14.
