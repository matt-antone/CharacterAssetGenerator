Performance arc

Charleston: a swinging four-count of kick-back, kick-forward with the arms counter-swinging against the legs. Frames 0-5 rock the weight back over the character-left foot with the arms crossed high, then the character-left knee folds up and forward as the character-right arm throws overhead — a big diagonal opposition, torso leaning character-right over the standing leg.

Frames 6-11 are the fastest part of the cycle: the folded character-left knee snaps through, the arms cut down past the hips and swing back up, and the hips twist character-right against the shoulders for the recoil. Frames 12-17 pass through the neutral centre — both feet briefly down, arms low and loose — and start the mirror half, the character-right knee lifting as the character-left arm opens out.

Frames 18-23 finish the mirror lift and return the weight to both feet in the open crossed-arm shape frame 0 holds, so the seam is one more swing rather than a stop. Keep the knee folds tight and the arm swing full-range; a small Charleston reads as a shuffle.

For the artist agent

  • This is a motion source: it controls motion only. Character scale, proportions, identity, view and prop hand come from the manifest and approved authorities, never from this video.
  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop at 12 fps, 24 frames. Loop seam: clean (frame 24 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Depth is explicit: every landmark in pts is [x, y, z] with z negative toward the camera and the hips at zero, and each frame carries a depth block plus a cue sentence wherever the legs overlap. Sort bones by mean z and draw the far ones first; never guess which limb is in front.
  • Grounded frames keep the supporting heel on the canonical baseline; frames marked airborne leave it only through a coherent takeoff, arc and landing.
  • Draw keys first (pink), then pilots (blue), then in-betweens from locked neighbours. The frame notes below are the per-frame instruction set.
