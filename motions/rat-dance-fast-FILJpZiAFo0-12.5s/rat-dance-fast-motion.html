Performance arc

The rat dance at performance speed: two mirrored halves, each a weight change onto one foot with the free leg crossing behind while the opposite arm folds up across the chest. The pulse is continuous — both heels stay lifted, the body never settles flat, and the knees stay soft throughout. Nothing in this loop holds still; the keys are direction changes, not poses.

Frames 0-5, the character-right half: weight starts on the character-left foot, crosses to the character-right on frame 2, and the character-left arm sweeps up across the torso to above shoulder height at frame 3 — the first hit, with the right hip and shoulder riding up under it. The arm drains back down through chest height to the hip by frame 5 as the hips counter-rotate character-right against the shoulders.

Frames 6-11 mirror it onto the other side: weight swings onto the character-left foot, the character-right leg crosses behind on frames 7-8, and the character-right arm rises across the body to shoulder height at frame 8 — the second hit. The head turns character-left from frame 10 and stays there through the recovery. Frames 12-15 restate the first hit, smaller and faster: left arm up across the chest at 12, dropping through 13-14, and frame 15 lands both feet down with the left arm swinging out to the side.

The loop seam is frame 15 into frame 0 and it reads clean: 15 is the weight rolling toward the character-right with the arms crossing low, 0 picks it up on the character-left foot mid-bounce. Carry the arm swing straight through the cut — it is the same pendulum, not a restart.

  • Every side is named character-left / character-right (the performer's own sides). Screen sides are never used for anatomy. The mirror button flips the drawing only; the text does not change.
  • Playback is loop at 8 fps, 16 frames. Loop seam: clean (frame 16 cuts back to frame 0).
  • Depth is explicit: every landmark in pts is [x, y, z] with z negative toward the camera and the hips at zero, and each frame carries a depth block plus a cue sentence wherever the legs overlap. Sort bones by mean z and draw the far ones first; never guess which limb is in front.
