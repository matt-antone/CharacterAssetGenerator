Performance arc

A continuation of the arms-up Nicki groove. Weight starts over the character-left foot with the arm out at shoulder height, the character-right foot lifts at frame 3, then the weight drifts through centre (frame 13) to the character-right foot (frame 15) and back.

Arms stay framed at shoulder height, elbows bent; the hips drive. Pingpong: frames 0 and 30 are grounded turnarounds.

  • Playback is loop · out and back at 8 fps, 31 frames. Loop seam: needs blend (frame 30 cuts back to frame 0).
