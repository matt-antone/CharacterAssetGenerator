Performance arc

A Nicki Minaj pose-and-sway with the arms held up. Arms stay at shoulder height with elbows bent throughout. The figure lifts the character-right foot at frame 1, returns to weight over the character-left, centres at frame 5, and holds weight over the character-right foot from frame 6 while the arm opens and closes.

Attitude over travel. Pingpong: frames 0 and 30 are grounded turnarounds.
