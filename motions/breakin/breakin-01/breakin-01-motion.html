Performance arc

An arm wave with the feet planted. Weight stays centred throughout; the wave travels through the arms held out to the side, rising from waist to chest to shoulder height by frames 4-6 and rolling back down through chest and waist.

The body is still; draw the elbows and wrists articulating the wave. Pingpong: frames 0 and 21 are grounded turnarounds.
