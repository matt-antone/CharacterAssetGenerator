Performance arc

A 1950s swing step with a kick. The figure hops between feet, character-right to character-left at frame 3 and back at frame 7, and the character-left arm swings up to overhead at frame 8 before coming down.

Straight loop, not pingpong: frame 18 cuts back to frame 0, both with weight on the character-right foot and the character-left foot lifted, so the seam is clean.
