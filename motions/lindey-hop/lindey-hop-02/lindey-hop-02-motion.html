Performance arc

A 1950s arm swing. It opens with the character-left arm overhead and straight, crossing the body, and holds it high through frame 3 while the weight shifts, then brings the arm down through shoulder and waist height and across the body by frame 6.

The back half is a low swing over the character-right foot. Pingpong: frames 0 and 13 are grounded turnarounds; frame 0 is the reach.
